I want a place to keep track of what I'm learning while I build things. Not just
the finished features, but the problems I ran into, the ideas I changed, and
what I understand better afterward.

One project that has taught me a lot is [Daisywheel
REAMade](https://github.com/Dewopalis/daisywheel-reamade), an attempt to make a
daisywheel-style on-screen keyboard work well with a controller. What began as
a prototype has grown into a real desktop app for handheld PCs and living-room
computers. Along the way I've had to think about KDE integration, controller
navigation, text editing, packaging, and how to make the interface usable from
the couch.

Working on it has shown me that a feature can look finished and still be hard to
use in practice. Testing on my own Linux desktop, noticing where the experience
felt awkward, and iterating from that feedback has been just as important as
writing the code.

I'll use this blog to write down those lessons as they happen, along with other
projects and things I'm curious about. This is the beginning; I'll improve the
site as I go.
