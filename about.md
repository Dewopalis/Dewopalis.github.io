Hi, I'm Dewald. This is where I keep notes on what I'm learning and making: the
small discoveries, the wrong turns, and the decisions that helped a project move
forward.

## So, what's a Dewopalis?

A spelling mistake.

When I was little, I was playing SimCity and needed a name for my city. I'd
heard the word *metropolis* before and thought it sounded pretty cool. There
was just one small problem: English is my second language, and I hadn't quite
figured out how to spell it yet.

So I did the obvious thing. I combined my name, Dewald, with my best
approximation of *metropolis*.

And thus, **Dewopalis** was born.

The spelling was wrong, the city almost definitely burned down, and somehow the
name stuck. At this point, I've been using it for so long that correcting it
would feel like the actual mistake.

*Pronounced: dewo-polis*

## A little about me

I have a soft spot for retro games, emulation, and the curious rabbit holes
around how software works. I like taking things apart conceptually, from old
game systems and Linux desktops to controller input, and seeing what I can
build or learn along the way. Sometimes that means a small experiment; sometimes
it grows into a much bigger project than I expected.

Lately, that project has been **Daisywheel REAmade**, a controller-first
on-screen keyboard for handheld PCs and living-room computers. It brings
together a few things I enjoy: making a familiar interaction work in a new
setting, tinkering with Linux, and improving the details through hands-on use.

I'm also starting **Open Golden Sun** (a working title): an early Godot rebuild
that aims to combine *Golden Sun* and *The Lost Age* into one continuous game,
with mod support built in from the outset. I'm interested in understanding how
the two games fit together and making room for people to experiment with them.
It's still at the scaffolding stage and not quite ready to share publicly, but
I'll write more about it as it grows.

This blog is a work in progress, just like the things I'll write about. You
can find the code for Daisywheel REAmade on
[GitHub](https://github.com/Dewopalis/daisywheel-reamade).
