Hi, I'm Dewald. This is where I keep notes on what I'm learning and making: the
small discoveries, the wrong turns, and the decisions that helped a project move
forward.

I have a soft spot for retro games, emulation, and the curious rabbit holes
around how software works. I like taking things apart conceptually—old game
systems, Linux desktops, controller input—and seeing what I can build or learn
along the way. Sometimes that means a small experiment; sometimes it grows into
a much bigger project than I expected.

Lately, that project has been **Daisywheel REAmade**, a controller-first
on-screen keyboard for handheld PCs and living-room computers. It brings
together a few things I enjoy: making a familiar interaction work in a new
setting, tinkering with Linux, and improving the details through hands-on use.

I'm also starting **Open Golden Sun** (a working title): an early Godot rebuild
that aims to combine *Golden Sun* and *The Lost Age* into one continuous game,
with mod support built in from the outset. I'm interested in understanding how
the two games fit together and making room for people to experiment with them.
It's still at the scaffolding stage, and I'll share more as it grows.

This blog is a work in progress, just like the things I'll write about. You can
find the code for Daisywheel REAmade on [GitHub](https://github.com/Dewopalis/daisywheel-reamade).
