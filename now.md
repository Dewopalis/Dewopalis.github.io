## What I'm working on

**Daisywheel REAmade** is my open-source, controller-first on-screen keyboard
for handheld PCs and living-room computers. I'm continuing to test it in real
desktop workflows and write down what I learn from building for Windows and
Linux. [See the project page]({{ '/projects/#daisywheel-reamade' | relative_url }}).

Alongside that, I'm building **Open Golden Sun** (a working title), an early
Godot rebuild that aims to combine *Golden Sun* and *The Lost Age* as one
continuous game, with mod support designed in from the outset. The repository
is at the scaffolding stage and isn't quite ready to share publicly; I'm
shaping the architecture and researching how the games fit together. [See the
project overview]({{ '/projects/#open-golden-sun' | relative_url }}).

I'm also exploring retro games, emulation, and the systems behind them, and I
hope to share those discoveries as I go.

## What's next

I want to keep improving the projects, capture more of the experiments and
unexpected problems along the way, and give each project a useful home on this
site.
