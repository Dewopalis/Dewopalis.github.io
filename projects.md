## Daisywheel REAmade
{: #daisywheel-reamade }

An open-source, controller-first on-screen keyboard for Windows and Linux. It
is designed for handheld PCs and living-room computers, where a controller is
often the input device already in your hands.

## Golden Sun decompilation

A side project exploring a decompilation of **Golden Sun**. I enjoy the

related notes as I publish more about the project.
